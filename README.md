### Scrum Master & Agile Practitioner · Team facilitation & continuous improvement

As a Scrum Master at Piano Analytics, I help my team improve how we work and deliver software. I also design and facilitate workshops for other teams to help them understand a shared problem and agree on concrete next steps.

### Senior Data Engineer · Real-time data systems, CI/CD & reliability

I build and operate real-time analytics pipelines at Piano Analytics. I develop Kafka-based services in Java and Kotlin, automate builds and deployments, monitor production with Datadog, and investigate incidents.

- **Data engineering** - Kafka, Kafka Connect, Java, Kotlin, gRPC
- **Delivery and operations** - AWS, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, Jenkins, Datadog
- **Software quality** - automated testing, property-based testing with jqwik, and incident postmortems
